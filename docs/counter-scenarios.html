Seven ways money reaches the counter

One ₱700 job order, followed through every way a customer can pay for it — what gets written on paper, what the system records, and what each report says afterwards. Every peso figure on this page was computed by the code itself, not worked out by hand.

Worked example ₱700 job order Receipts handwritten on BIR booklets Verified by scripts/verify-sales-audit.ts

The one rule underneath all seven

A receipt is issued for what was handed over, and books exactly that

Not for the job's total. A ₱700 job paid ₱230 now and ₱470 on release is two documents — ₱230 of sales today, ₱470 of sales on the day it ships. They add up to the job, and the shop's books follow the money rather than an accrual nobody writes down.

every receipt against a job → Σ = the job's total

Two consequences worth stating, because both are easy to get backwards. A downpayment is a sale of its own amount — it is tagged so the day's log shows who is coming back, but the tag moves no total. And a Collection Receipt is never a sale — it settles an invoice whose revenue was already booked, so counting it again would report the same peso twice.

One job stays on one series throughout. A walk-in job runs on JO slips start to finish; a VAT customer's job runs on VAT invoices. The counter refuses to mix them, which is why a JO slip cannot be followed by a sales invoice on the same job.

The five documents

Each one is a leaf out of a numbered booklet, except the DR, which is its own paper.

JO
Job Order Slip
Cash receipt for a walk-in. Books the amount paid. May be tagged as a downpayment.
IN
Sales Invoice — VAT
VAT backed out of the amount at 12%. Shares its serial series with the Charge Invoice.
NV
Sales Invoice — Non-VAT
Its own series. Carries no VAT; the whole amount is reported as vatable sales.
IN
Charge Invoice
Sells on credit. Books revenue immediately and creates the receivable, with a due date.
CR
Collection Receipt
Acknowledges cash against a Charge Invoice. Never revenue — the invoice already booked it.
DR
Delivery Receipt
Proof the work left the building. Sets delivered quantities; issues no serial from a sales booklet.

A Non-VAT sale reports its whole amount as vatable sales with zero VAT — that is what makes the two series summable side by side without the tax figures colliding. The NV booklet runs its own numbering, so a Non-VAT sale can never eat a serial out of the IN line.

Credit — delivered on a DR, paid later

No downpayment. The work goes out on a Delivery Receipt, and the sales document is written only when the customer comes to settle. Scenarios 4, 5 and 6 differ only in which booklet that final document comes from.

The Unbilled & Backlog report is currently the only place that money is visible. Making DR-only debt age properly is the next piece of work on this track.

The 2% is computed on the VAT-exclusive ₱625.00, not the ₱700 gross — computing it on the gross over-withholds and leaves the invoice permanently short. A government or LGU customer withholds a second tax on the same base: 5% VAT on Form 2306, kept apart because the two are claimed on different returns.

What the counter refuses

Each of these is a rule the system enforces rather than trusts, with the mistake it prevents.

The counter refusesBecause
An invoice on a job that already has a JO slip One job stays on one series. Mixing them would put the same sale in two document lines.
A JO slip on a job that has already been invoiced The mirror of the above.
Billing more than the job's unbilled remainder A ₱700 job already billed ₱230 can only be billed ₱470 more. Receipts must sum to the job.
Collecting more than is outstanding A collection settles an invoice; there is nothing to settle beyond its open balance.
Two cashiers collecting the same last peso The balance is re-checked inside the same statement that writes it, so the second one fails.
Issuing past the last leaf of a booklet All fifty leaves stay accountable. A used-up booklet closes rather than overflowing.
Deleting a spoiled receipt It is voided in place, keeping its serial, so the booklet still reconciles.
A cashier voiding their own receipt Cancellation needs a supervisor — the person who took the money is not the one who writes it off.
